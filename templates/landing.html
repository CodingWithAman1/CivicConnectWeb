Seamless Lifecycle

How CivicConnect Works

Transparent civic governance from photo upload to citizen verification.

Step 1
Report & AI Scan

Citizen captures an issue photo. Gemini Vision auto-detects title, category, hazard severity, and department.

Step 2
Cluster & Route

Spatial algorithms detect duplicates within 300 meters. The ticket is queued to the responsible municipal department.

Step 3
Resolve & Proof

City crews or local NGOs take the task, execute the repair, and upload photographic resolution proof.

Step 4
Verify & Close

Multimodal AI cross-checks before & after images. The citizen confirms resolution satisfaction to close the ticket.
