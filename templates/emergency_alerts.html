Citizen Safety Action Protocols

Flash Floods & Waterlogging

Never drive through flooded underpasses or walk through moving water. Avoid basement parking during red-alert storms and move to higher elevations.

Exposed Live Wires

Stay at least 10 meters away from snapped wires or sagging power cables. Report immediately as an Emergency SOS and alert pedestrians in the vicinity.

Gas Leak / Toxic Spills

Evacuate upwind of the odor. Do not turn electrical switches or vehicles on or off. Extinguish all open flames and call emergency responders immediately.

Structural Collapse

Evacuate compromised structures immediately. Keep clear of overhead balconies, power poles, and large trees during severe storms and seismic events.
